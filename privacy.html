🎨 Coloring Fun — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Coloring Fun is a tap-to-color game for kids and families. We built it to work entirely on your own device, with no ads and no data collection. This page explains exactly what that means.

What information we collect

When you first open the app, we may ask for a name and country so we can show it on your local clear-time record. This information — along with your game progress, scores, and settings — is stored only in your device's browser storage (localStorage). It is never sent to us, to any server, or to any third party. We have no server, no database, and no account system, so we simply have no way to see this information even if we wanted to.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

Coloring Fun does not show ads, does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Works offline

After the first load, the app works fully offline. No internet connection is required to play.

Children's privacy

Coloring Fun is designed to be enjoyed by kids together with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children, and as noted above, any name or country you enter stays only on your own device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
