🎨 Coloring Fun — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Coloring Fun is a tap-to-color game for kids and families. We built it with no ads and no tracking. This page explains exactly what information the app uses and, for the one optional feature that leaves your device (the global ranking), exactly what is shared.

What stays only on your device

Your game progress, scores, settings, and the nickname/flag you pick are stored only in your device's browser storage (localStorage). This never leaves your device and is never sent to us or any third party. We have no account system and no way to see this information.

What is shared publicly (only if you choose to)

After clearing all 10 levels of a mode, you can optionally save your time to a public global ranking so players can compare fastest clear times. If you choose to do this, we send only: the nickname you typed (not your real name — please don't use your real name!), the flag emoji you picked (not a precise location), your clear time, and the date. This is stored on a database (Firebase, by Google) and is visible to anyone who opens the ranking screen. You can always tap "Skip" instead and this will never be sent anywhere. We do not ask for or store real names, addresses, birthdates, or any other identifying information.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

Coloring Fun does not show ads, does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Works offline

Except for loading/saving a ranking record, the app works fully offline. No internet connection is required to play.

Children's privacy

Coloring Fun is designed to be enjoyed by kids together with a parent or guardian. Before the ranking feature can be used, a simple "parent check" screen asks an adult to solve a quick math problem. We do not knowingly collect any real personal information from children — the ranking only ever stores a self-chosen nickname and flag emoji, never a real name, address, or other identifying detail.
